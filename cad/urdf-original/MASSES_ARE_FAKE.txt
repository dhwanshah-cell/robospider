The original Onshape URDF export. Every <mass> is a unit-density artifact:
mass numerically equals the mesh volume in m^3. Summed, the entire robot
comes to 1.06 GRAMS.

It also references the collision-proxy meshes, so both the masses and the
geometry are wrong.

Use ../../sim/cubebot.urdf instead — same kinematics, real masses and
inertias, no mesh dependencies.
